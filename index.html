How to Play

Objective

Your dot orbits a circular track that has two concentric lanes, an inner one and an outer one. Colored segments appear on the track ahead of you. Survive as long as possible while racking up points — the track never stops accelerating, so the real skill is reading the track early and reacting cleanly.

Controls

One input does everything: tap the screen, click, or press Space to switch between the inner and outer lane. There's no other control — every segment on the track is dealt with by being in the right lane (or the wrong one) at the right moment.

Reading the track

The lane you're currently on is highlighted with a soft amber glow on its ring, so you always know where you are at a glance. Segments are drawn ahead of you on either the inner or outer lane — watch which lane they sit on, not just their color.

Red — Obstacle

Blocks one lane. If you're in the same lane as a red segment when you reach it, you lose a life. Switch to the other lane before it arrives to dodge it safely — a clean dodge gives a small confirming tick sound.

Green — Target

Switch into its lane to score. Points aren't all-or-nothing: every segment shows a small solid dot at its exact center — that's the sweet spot. The closer to that dot you cross it, the more of the segment's full point value you earn; clipping the faded edge still scores, just less. The same dot marks the sweet spot on every bonus segment below, so you can always judge your timing by eye.

Every few targets hit (8 in Easy, 6 in Hard) you earn a bonus extra life, up to your difficulty's life cap.

Blue — Slow

Switch into its lane to trigger a temporary slowdown. A countdown ring appears at the center of the track showing exactly how long the reprieve lasts. It's a breather, not a reset: the underlying difficulty ramp keeps building in the background and picks back up the moment the ring empties.

Purple — Extra Life (rare)

The rarest segment. Switch into its lane for an instant +2 lives, up to your difficulty's cap. Don't plan around it — it's a welcome surprise, not a reliable resource.

💣 Orange — Bomb

Switch into its lane to detonate it. Every red obstacle currently on the track is destroyed in a burst of particles, instantly and safely — a clean way to defuse a dangerous stretch ahead. It doesn't score points on its own; its value is the breathing room it buys you.

Lives & difficulty

Easy — start with 4 lives, stockpile up to 8, gentler speed ramp.
Hard — start with 2 lives, stockpile up to 4, steeper speed ramp from the very first second.

Run out of lives and it's game over — your final score and difficulty are shown, and you can submit your name to the global leaderboard.

Leaderboard

Scores are tracked separately per difficulty. Check the Global Leaderboard from the start screen, or from the game-over screen right after a run, to see how you stack up.
